# Changelog

[Česky](CHANGELOG.cs.md)

## v1.9.2 – 27 Nov 2026
- **Desktop updates without the browser:** the app downloads the new installer itself, starts it with
  **Install and close the app** and quits so the installer can replace it.

## v1.9.1 – 27 Nov 2026
- **Familiars get their Bestiary statistics** (abilities, size, speed, attacks) when you pick the kind.
  Familiars created in 1.9.0 get them on the next settings change.

## v1.9.0 – 27 Nov 2026 – Companions
- **157 animal companions** – 140 more from other Paizo books (dinosaurs, elephants, sharks…), searchable picker.
- **Familiars** – 89 kinds; the master's bonus (e.g. cat +3 Stealth, toad +3 HP) counts on the master's sheet,
  natural armor, Int and special abilities follow the master's level.
- **Eidolons** – summoner and unchained summoner progression, base forms, subtypes and 79 evolutions with evolution points.

## v1.8.0 – 27 Nov 2026 – Prestige classes
- **118 prestige classes** (Eldritch Knight, Mystic Theurge, Arcane Trickster…) with BAB, saves, hit dice and skills
  from their tables.
- Prestige classes that advance spellcasting raise the **caster level** of the class you choose.
- Requirements are checked like feat prerequisites; strict mode blocks unmet ones.

## v1.7.0 – 27 Nov 2026 – Archetypes
- **1,252 class archetypes** with full text, picked below each class.
- Archetypes change the class: replaced abilities, subclasses and bonus feats disappear, some lose or change spellcasting
  (another spell list, fewer spells, extra spells), a few add new abilities.
- Two archetypes replacing the same feature are reported.

## v1.6.1 – 27 Nov 2026
- Menu **Databases & updates** with the app update section on top.

## v1.6.0 – 26 Nov 2026 – App updates
- **Update the app from GitHub Releases** – Android downloads and installs the new APK, desktop the installer.
  Before installing you can export your characters and inventories.
- The Android APK is now signed with a stable key (older versions need a one-time reinstall).

## v1.5.0 – 26 Nov 2026 – Class abilities
- Hexes, revelations and subclass powers of every level (domains, bloodlines, schools…) with uses per day.
- More subclasses: witch patron, shaman spirit (including wandering spirit), bloodrager bloodline, psychic discipline,
  warpriest blessings, inquisitor domains and inquisitions.
- **Alternate racial traits** (~630) – replaced traits stop counting for feat prerequisites.
